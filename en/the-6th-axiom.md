# The Sixth Axiom of Meaning

## Text

**Sixth Axiom: Meaning is price.**

> **Meaning = price = supply–demand equilibrium (in context c).** Demand side = the necessity of being selected; supply side = the abundance of alternative expressions.

**Everyday anchor: one word worth a thousand pieces of gold.** A word's price is its meaning — its scarcity and necessity set the price. Supporting lines: **what is rare is dear** (supply side: low abundance, high price), **half a sentence is too much when minds don't meet** (context mismatch, price fails to form), **playing the lute to a cow** (zero demand — no price, no meaning). The three lines exactly cover supply, demand, and context — naive intuition welded "meaning" to "price" long ago.

**Position of the axiom.** The Sixth Axiom is the **meaning criterion** of meta-science, the seventh link of the seven-link chain:

> Zeroth Axiom (**entry of existence**) → First Axiom (**judgment of correctness**) → Second Axiom (**direction of approach**) → Third Axiom (**multi-agent dynamics**) → Fourth Axiom (**openness criterion**) → Fifth Axiom (**social criterion**) → Sixth Axiom (**meaning criterion**).

It stipulates: meaning is measured not by subjective feeling or metaphysical "value" but by **price** (the supply–demand equilibrium in expressive space).

**Core meaning.** Three strokes:

- **Meaning is not an intrinsic property but a structural relation**: a piece of information has no fixed "meaning" of its own — its meaning depends on its position in the whole expressive system (its price), just as a commodity's price depends on its position in the market.
- **Meaning does not require consciousness**: meaning depends on position in expressive space, and price is determined by the system's structure — no conflict with "cognition is a component of existence": pricing happens inside expressive space and needs no external observer.
- **Meaning is distributed**: as market prices are dispersed across countless transactions, meaning is dispersed across the whole expressive space — no symbol "holds" meaning; meaning is an emergent property at the system level.

**Meaning is a category; price is an annotation.** Meaning = (whether an expression produces a selection tendency), judged case by case as a binary (in the First Axiom's sense); **price (degree of meaning) is an engineering-layer annotation** (outline precedent: "game solver = efficiency annotation source"). Three common quantities point in different directions and must not be conflated into a definition:

| Quantity | Direction | Engineering-layer use |
|---|---|---|
| Frequency P(x\|c) | High frequency = high demand | Demand-side annotation |
| Information −log P | Scarce = high surprise | Scarcity-side annotation |
| Entropy H(x\|c) | Low entropy = high predictability | Predictability-side annotation |

Equilibrium price is jointly determined by supply and demand — by frequency, function words like "the" would be dearest; by scarcity, rare words would be dearest; neither is the equilibrium price. Frequency, information, and entropy alone are each mere annotations.

**Generalization source: the principle of supply and demand.** In competitive markets, price is set by supply–demand equilibrium. Hayek's three insights:

1. **Price is information**: it compresses vast dispersed information — scarcity, preferences, costs;
2. **Price is incentive**: it guides behavior — high prices spur supply and restrain demand;
3. **Price is self-organization**: with no central planner, prices emerge spontaneously from countless individual exchanges.

Mapped onto this axiom: supply = the abundance of alternative expressions in expressive space; demand = the necessity of being selected in a context; **meaning = the supply–demand equilibrium price**. This elevates the theory from economics to a general theory of meaning: the price mechanism allocates not only resources but meaning.

**The science quadruple.**

| Quadrant | Commitment | Delivery | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Science** | Yes | Yes | Meaning is determined by verifiable selection tendency (price) |
| **Philosophy** | No | No | Price fails to form (love-of-wisdom freedom law: philosophy has no right or wrong) |
| **Religion** | Yes | No | Delivery = zero → the price is false (the commitment's pricing is missing) |
| **Engineering** | No | Yes | Claims no universal truth, produces measurable effects (an effective price) |

The definitions live on the commitment/delivery axes (the four cells themselves); the meaning reading is a **case-by-case inferred judgment** (not a definition) — philosophy's "no price" hangs on the love-of-wisdom boundary law (no price on the uncertain side).

**The loop with the earlier axioms.**

- **To the Zeroth Axiom**: difference produces choice; choice produces price — difference is the **expressive-structural basis** of meaning. Information without distinguishable difference produces no choice; its price is zero.
- **To the First Axiom**: only a complete symbol system (nothing unsayable) can produce accurate price signals — the First Axiom judges a meaning system's "correctness"; the Sixth Axiom defines meaning's "substance." Note that "the inexpressible does not enter meaning judgment" (case not accepted, same treatment as the liar sentence): there is no "distortion" to speak of, only no "filing."
- **To the Second Axiom**: a meaning system tending toward completeness = the direction of more accurate price signals (language evolution: fewer symbols expressing more distinctions). Equilibrium is a **cross-section** of price (an engineering-layer snapshot); meaning formation is a game process (Third Axiom: turbulence is the pricing process itself) — supply–demand equilibrium is only an operable approximation.
- **To the Third Axiom**: consensus prices are institutions that unblock meaning — shared language, scientific terminology, and legal concepts are all infrastructure lowering meaning's transaction costs. The collapse of a meaning system = chaotic price signals = soaring meaning transaction costs.
- **To the Fourth Axiom**: **unpriced space = the freedom of meaning** (correct ignorance: the source of creativity); the already-consensed symbol system = the order of meaning. If everything were priced, expressive space would rigidify — same as "water too clear harbors no fish."
- **To the Fifth Axiom**: **meaning = price and justice = efficiency are methodological kin** (operationalizing metaphysical concepts into testable criteria), without mutual reduction — truth, good, beauty each have their criterion (truth = correctness / good = efficiency / goodness = cooperation / beauty = competitiveness); price governs only meaning.

**Pruning theorem: prune pseudo-meaning.** What pruning cuts is "priceless meaning," i.e. **meaning that does not take price as its form** — pseudo-meaning: claiming significance while producing no selection tendency. Isomorphic to "pseudo-freedom/pseudo-justice"; it does not cut meaning itself. Many metaphysical assertions and ideological slogans claim "profound meaning" yet produce no measurable selection tendency — occupying expressive space and jamming pricing. **Pruning pseudo-meaning = releasing expressive space**, in the same direction as unblocking and friction reduction.

**Case table.**

| Object | Verdict | Disposition |
|---|---|---|

| LLM conditional-probability fitting | A pricing model (engineering layer): fits the price function, not the meaning criterion itself | Back-reference to the Fourth Axiom case (the hallucination case); the understanding criterion belongs to First Axiom completeness |

**Self-reference and scale-freeness.** This axiom refers to itself and applies to itself:

> **Scientific layer**: meaning is price (content unchanged). **Text layer**: The Principle's own meaning = its price — the **tendency to be selected**: counterexamples are checkable (low testing cost), it is cited, it is absorbed ("if something better appears, absorb it" is itself the pricing mechanism of the meaning market), credit accumulates (the First value is the price of commitment).

Conjectures are **unpriced space** (the meaning-layer form of Fourth Axiom freedom). Mutable self-reference is forbidden: price must not be used to redefine "correctness" (the criterion belongs to the First Axiom) — a best-selling wrong assertion is not correct; price judges meaning only, not truth or goodness. **The axiom is scale-free**: price contains no scale parameter — applicable to words, concepts, theories, institutions, any expressive space (matter/mind neutral).

**Summary.** The Sixth Axiom does three things:

1. **Operationalizes meaning**: metaphysical concept → testable criterion (price); meaning is a category, price an engineering-layer annotation;
2. **Unifies the criterion method for meaning and value**: meaning = price and justice = efficiency are both meta-scientific operationalizations, each governing its own criterion;
3. **Provides an analytic frame for understanding and alignment**: an LLM's "understanding" is price-function fitting (engineering layer); the meaning criterion lies in expressive-space structure — AI interpretation belongs to the application/conjecture layer.

### 2. Detailed note on the supply–demand lineage

Hayek (1945): the price mechanism transmits the most complex social information in the minimal form (a single number) — the miracle of dispersed knowledge. Equilibrium price is a **cross-section** of price (an engineering-layer snapshot); meaning formation is a **game process** (Third Axiom: turbulence is the pricing process itself) — language evolution and scientific-paradigm shifts are disequilibrium, path-dependent pricing histories. Supply–demand equilibrium is only an operable approximation, not the static essence of meaning. Limitations and responses:

- Non-competitive meaning formation → Games theorem (Third Axiom): blockage can keep "wrong meaning" alive for a long time;
- Path dependence → Second Axiom: meaning evolution overall tends toward higher efficiency (more accurate price signals);
- Non-rational factors → Fourth Axiom: unpriced space is the source of creativity; not everything is "rational choice."

### 3. Note on the demotion of formalization

Formulas like a degree-of-meaning M(x,c) (conditional-probability ratios, cross-entropy) are **engineering-layer annotation sources** and must not enter the definitional layer. Three common quantities point in different directions:

| Quantity | Direction | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Frequency P(x\|c) | High frequency = high demand | Demand-side annotation |
| Information −log P | Scarce = high surprise | Scarcity-side annotation |
| Entropy H(x\|c) | Low entropy = high predictability | Predictability-side annotation |

**They must not be conflated into a definition**: by frequency, "the" is dearest; by scarcity, rare words are dearest; the equilibrium price is jointly determined by supply and demand. Trustworthy deduction must be based on axioms: formalization that does not trace back to axioms is mere notation.

### 4. The four semantic traditions for contrast

| Tradition | Core question | The Sixth Axiom's response |
|---|---|---|
| Reference theory | How words refer to the world | Reference = price (which possibilities it distinguishes) |
| Use theory | Meaning is use | Use is the pricing mechanism (frequency forms price) |
| Verification theory | Meaning is verifiability | Verifiable = measurable price difference |
| Model theory | Meaning is interpretation in a model | Interpretation function = pricing function (the symbol's position in the model) |

The four traditions are the philosophical quadrant's genealogy; the Sixth Axiom **replaces** them with price (the certain side) — the same treatment the Fifth Axiom gives the philosophical quadrant: replace, not synthesize.

### 5. Information economics and mechanism design

General equilibrium: the whole expressive space is a meaning market in which all symbol prices are simultaneously determined and interdependent. Information economics: the price of information depends on whether it can change a decision-maker's posterior probabilities. Mechanism design: designing good meaning-formation mechanisms (language, scientific paradigms, legal terminology) = designing market mechanisms that generate accurate price signals — the Sixth Axiom's direct engineering-layer application (parallel to the Fifth Axiom's institutional design).

### 6. Credit = the price of commitment

The First value (credit) is **the price of commitment**: the commitment/delivery record prices commitments. The price mechanism allocates resources, the credit mechanism allocates commitments — together they allocate meaning: the Fifth Axiom's two infrastructures (credit + price) are welded to the Sixth Axiom here. "If something better appears, absorb it" is itself a pricing act of the meaning market: better texts being absorbed = the price signal working.

### 7. Archived meaning reading of the quadruple

Science = commitment and delivery (meaning determined by price); philosophy = no commitment, no delivery (price fails to form); religion = commitment without delivery (false price); engineering = no commitment but delivery (effective price). **The definitions live on the commitment/delivery axes (the four cells themselves); the meaning reading is a case-by-case inferred judgment** (not a definition) — "philosophy has no meaning" should hang on the love-of-wisdom freedom law (philosophy has no right or wrong) and the boundary law (no price on the uncertain side); the phrasing is changed from assertion to verdict.

### 8. LLMs and embeddings (conjecture layer)

What an LLM learns is the conditional probability distribution of words in the corpus — price-function fitting (an engineering-layer pricing model). Mind the two layers:

- **A pricing model ≠ the meaning criterion**: fitting the price function is an engineering-layer capability; the meaning criterion lies in expressive-space structure (a categorial judgment);
- **AI understanding/alignment readings belong to the conjecture layer**: "understanding = mastering the price function" and "providing an operable definition for AI alignment" are extensions of the outline's conjecture section (the alignment principle), not direct corollaries of the Sixth Axiom.
