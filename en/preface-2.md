### 1. A Fundamental Decoupling: Science vs Science as Human Cognition

For a long time, academia has sunk into a profound intellectual confusion: it takes the instrumental limitations humans manufactured at a particular historical stage to be the ultimate barriers of the objective world, and mistakes the stagnation of human linguistic construction for the twilight of reason.

To understand *The Principle*, one must first make a fundamental distinction:

1. **Science (ontology)**:
   - It is an objective, eternal, self-closed absolute system.
   - It obeys the **Law of Scientific Truth** (scientific principles must be absolutely correct) and the **Law of Scientific Eternity** (scientific principles must not change). Within scientific ontology, **correctness is completeness**; truth and provability are naturally equivalent; there is no "unproven truth," nor any mystic blank space.
2. **Science as human cognition**:
   - It is a **stage-local approximation** produced by humans in the course of evolution, constrained by biological brain structure, computational bandwidth, and the politics of the academic community.
   - It **is often incomplete**, full of bias, noise, and compromise.

That science as human cognition is incomplete in no way means that scientific ontology is incomplete.

The Principle's **absorption commitment** to "superior theories of the future" exists precisely to shatter the dogma of self-enclosure by the human academic community: acting as a Darwinian selection mechanism spanning eras, it forcibly drives human cognition to converge on scientific ontology.

### 3. Expelling Gödel: Stripping It of Its Scientific Status

In the temple of so-called "modern philosophy of mathematics," Gödel's incompleteness theorems are enshrined as the most sacred totem of agnosticism.

**The Principle explicitly declares: on the basis of the First Axiom, Gödel's theorems are not accepted, and their scientific status is not acknowledged.**

This is not an ostrich policy, but precise surgery on the orthodox community's academic privilege:
1. **Definitional power, not ontology**: Gödel's so-called "arithmetical truths that cannot be exhausted" owe their "truth" entirely to the privileged definitions and semantic monopoly of an external metalanguage. Once "truth = provability" is established, the so-called "true but unprovable Gödel sentences" are refused entry at the level of grammatical well-formedness checks — they are **ill-formed expressions**, not an abyss of mathematics.
2. **A local tool, not science**: at most it is a local engineering obstacle within specific restricted systems (finite mechanical checking, specific diagonalization rules); it is in no way "the boundary of science."
3. **Technical assault, not philosophical chatter**: the Principle never shrinks from technical engagement; it launches the **Hilbert process**, and within the formal systems its opponents accept, uses "Gödel-world minimization" ($G/H \to 0$) and fine-grained control of self-referential boundaries to complete the technical clearance on the pure-mathematics battlefield.
