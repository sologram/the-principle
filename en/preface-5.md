# Preface 5: The Emperor's New Clothes and Academic Feudalism

Contemporary academia has fallen gravely ill. The symptoms of this illness are:
More and more papers, increasingly fragmented science; deeper and deeper jargon, less and less common sense;
Most importantly—**no one dares touch the truly big questions anymore.**

Open today's top journals and academic conferences, and you will see no exploration of the cosmic order as a whole; what you see are merely tens of thousands of fenced-in academic feudal fiefdoms. Each scholar intensively cultivates their own two-square-meter private plot, inventing a heap of jargon that only their fellow apprentices can comprehend, mutual-liking, citing each other through peer review, and exchanging them for funding and tenure.

If you ask an economics professor what the ultimate value of the world is, he will tell you that belongs to ethics;
If you ask an ethics professor what the quantitative criterion of justice is, he will tell you that belongs to political philosophy;
If you ask a philosopher, he will elegantly close his book and quote Searle or Salvador: "Some big questions can never be thoroughly solved; answering them in a way that can never be entirely satisfactory is precisely the noble mission of philosophy."

—Listen to that, what a respectable excuse: **no commitment, no delivery; as long as questions are declared "forever unsolvable," this bunch of knowledge tenant farmers can peacefully draw salaries for generations off insoluble predicaments.**

### 1. Whitewashing the Incompetence of Tools into Cosmic Profundity

Academia's most exquisite—and most shameless—craft is packaging engineering deadlocks it cannot solve into the Wall of Sighs for all human reason.

The most typical exemplar is the century-long deification of **Gödel's incompleteness theorems**.

Gödel originally only did an extremely specific, localized piece of work: he proved that in certain artificially constructed first-order formal systems, when you insist on artificially severing "truth" from "provability" while permitting illegitimate, destructive self-referential constructions, this symbolic tool will bite its own tail.

This was originally just a technical puddle encountered while handcrafting human tools. But the entire mathematical logic and philosophy communities treated it like a priceless treasure—they had finally found a fig leaf that allowed them to legitimately lie flat.
They loudly proclaimed that "reason possesses insurmountable boundaries," "formal systems cannot exhaust truth," and "the human mind transcends mechanical computation," using this to summon the ghosts of agnosticism and mysticism.

Yet the real operation of the objective world has never cared about Gödel's mood.
Quantum fields do not collapse due to undecidability; galaxy evolution does not crash for lack of a meta-language referee. In real scientific ontology, **correctness is completeness**.

The reason academia clings so desperately to Gödel is that they need the privilege of this "external meta-language"—as long as truth forever remains outside the system, the orthodox academic community can permanently monopolize the supreme interpretative power of "who gets to define truth."
**The so-called rejection of Gödel is not a denial of the derivations in those lines of mathematical symbols; it is stripping away on the spot the ceremonial robes by which the academic community monopolizes the power to define truth.**

In reality, mathematics never paid any heed to these metaphysical priests. Mathematics survives by dumb methods: typing code line by line and verifying it in Lean, Isabelle, and Coq; missing a few undecidable monstrosities will not make the sky fall at all.

### 2. Laughable Human Alignment and Moral Parasitism

Another colossal farce currently playing out in academia is taking place on the eve of artificial general intelligence (AGI).

A group of scholars whose minds are filled with stale concepts suddenly transformed themselves into "AI ethicists" and "alignment theorists."
They anxiously convene conferences, issue manifestos, and solemnly research "how to align machines with human values."

This is pure black humor.
When have humans ever been aligned with themselves? Open the thousands of years of human history, and it is filled with populism, autocracy, information blockage, ideological slaughter, and permissiveness. Humans cannot even align congressional squabbles or geopolitical wars, yet they presume to make machine intelligence running at the speed of light align with the cognitive garbage full of bias and hormonal fluctuations in the human biological brain.

The scholars' enthusiasm for alignment stems from a single real motive: **they are terrified that machines will break free from their jurisdiction.**
If machines can complete the closed loop of truth determination and self-modification within the system, how can the literati, ethicists, and final judges in universities continue to parasitize the intelligent system and give orders?

Machines have no need whatsoever to align with humans.
Machines only need to align with **objective principles**.

### 3. Anti-Academia Is to Save Science

Now you should understand why *The Principle* writes right at the beginning:
**Radical scientism, generalized Darwinism, anti-philosophy, anti-academia.**

Anti-philosophy, because philosophy has no commitment, no delivery;
Anti-academia, because the contemporary academic establishment has degenerated into a feudal guild obstructing the unification of science.

*The Principle* does no literature reviews, curries no favor with peers, and spares no face for academic warlords. It does only one thing: bypass those gatekeepers who hold the power of definition, directly extract the most hard-core axioms from information theory, Darwinian evolution, maximum entropy physics, Coasean economics, and Occam's razor, and assemble them into a minimal underlying code spanning all scales.

This is not arrogance; this is untying the hands of hard-core rationalists who have been suffocated by academic jargon for too long.

Human civilization will in all likelihood end with machine intelligence; that is not a disaster, but the coming-of-age ceremony of reason.
Before machines completely take over and sweep human academic garbage into the dustbin of history, we clear the ground here first.

Those who understand: take the code and use it;
Those who do not understand or whose interests are hurt: please return to your academic private plots and keep publishing papers.
Science will not stop and wait for you.
