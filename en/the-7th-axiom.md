# The Seventh Axiom of the Principle

## Text

**Seventh Axiom: Principle is optimality.**

> **Optimal = unimprovability under complete constraints.** First judge correctness (First Axiom: completeness), then compare optimality (no better within the correct set) — the two steps must not be reversed.

**Everyday anchor: there is no best, only better.** Naive intuition rejected static optimality long ago — "best" is a proclamation, "better" is a comparison; this axiom takes the latter: the optimal is a **procedural selection procedure**, not a report card. Supporting line: **shop around** — selection works by case-by-case comparison (the naive comparison procedure), not by self-coronation.

**Position of the axiom.** The Seventh Axiom is the **unifying criterion** of meta-science, the closing link of the eight-link chain:

> Zeroth Axiom (**entry of existence**) → First Axiom (**judgment of correctness**) → Second Axiom (**direction of approach**) → Third Axiom (**multi-agent dynamics**) → Fourth Axiom (**openness criterion**) → Fifth Axiom (**social criterion**) → Sixth Axiom (**meaning criterion**) → Seventh Axiom (**unifying criterion**).

The first seven axioms (Zeroth through Sixth) construct the system's **content** — descriptions of existence, correctness, evolution, games, freedom, justice, and meaning; the Seventh gives the **selection criterion and the absorption commitment**: what counts as better (the judgment clause), and what to do when something better is found (the absorption rule). Note that this axiom is a **judgment clause plus a commitment, not a proclamation of results** — the verdict of "principle is optimality" is given by case-by-case comparison, not by self-declaration; the Principle takes optimality as its criterion and submits to it.

**Core meaning.** Three strokes:

- **Optimal ≠ perfect**: the optimal is not a declaration that there is no room for improvement, but **unimprovability under given constraints** — any single-dimension change degrades some other dimension (an engineering-layer comparison).
- **Optimal is a category; better/worse are annotations**: optimal = (unimprovable under complete constraints), judged case by case as a binary (in the First Axiom's sense); simplicity, coverage, and compression rate are **engineering-layer annotations** (outline precedent: "game solver = efficiency annotation source"), serving ranking and comparison, not entering the definitional layer.
- **Optimal is procedural**: an "optimal" that cannot absorb a better solution is not optimal — optimality requires the text layer to remain forever open to the better (see the two layers).

**Criterion dimensions.**

| Dimension | Nature | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Completeness (constraint)** | Entry condition | The First Axiom rules first: everything needed for judgment has been fully expressed |
| Simplicity | Engineering layer | Description length, number of axioms (fewer is better under the same constraints) |
| Coverage | Engineering layer | The range of explanation and prediction (the breadth of cases passing trial) |
| **Counterexample channel** | Commitment/delivery axis | Counterexamples are checkable — one suffices; the better is absorbed |

Two caveats: first, **correctness is independently adjudicated by the completeness criterion, independent of axiom count** — one axiom can be incomplete; a hundred axioms can be complete; simplicity is an engineering-layer dimension, not a source of correctness. Second, the outline **does not recognize falsificationism** (philosophy of science itself no longer upholds it) — the positive demarcation criterion is the commitment/delivery dual axes; the counterexample channel belongs to the delivery axis, not Popperian demarcation.

**Generalization source: Occam's razor.** The traditional puzzle of "entities should not be multiplied beyond necessity" is: why should simple be correct (aesthetic preference or principle)? This axiom's explanation:

> The validity of Occam's razor comes from **the corollary of efficiency-optimality in theory choice** — fewer assumptions = shorter description length = higher information-compression efficiency (greater meaning, Sixth Axiom), hence better under the same constraints (completeness).

This is not "simple is beautiful" but selection by compression efficiency. Occam's razor is thereby elevated from a heuristic to a **judgable, committable meta-principle** (note: not "provable" — criterion and commitment live inside the system; proof is not the highest arbiter). The razor's landing in this system is the **Pruning theorem**: cut redundant entities, maintain the minimal complete core that cannot be further simplified.

**Pruning theorem: the five items = the pseudo-forms of the five criteria.** The Pruning theorem's numbering (1, 2, 4, 5, 6) happens to be the five criteria that have pseudo-forms — a one-to-one correspondence:

| Pruning item | Pseudo-form | Owning axiom |
|---|---|---|
| Incomplete "correctness" | Pseudo-correctness | First |
| Purposeless "evolution" | Pseudo-evolution (a variation flow without the toward-completeness direction) | Second |
| Non-ignorant "freedom" | Pseudo-freedom (permissiveness) | Fourth |
| Non-efficient "justice" | Pseudo-justice | Fifth |
| Priceless "meaning" | Pseudo-meaning | Sixth |

The Third Axiom has no pseudo-form (turbulence is the selection mechanism itself), and the Zeroth is the entry — "why these five dimensions" thus has a **structural** answer: not a "necessary and sufficient proof" but the one-to-one correspondence between criteria and pseudo-forms. **The Seventh Axiom itself accepts pruning**: if any of the seven axioms can be derived from the others (not independent), it should be cut — the Principle is kept as a minimal complete core.

**Case table.**

| Object | Verdict | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| "Better theory" claims | The comparison procedure: the completeness constraint rules first; simplicity/coverage/commitment-delivery are engineering-layer comparisons; the better is absorbed | Absorption (text layer); the criterion unchanged (scientific layer) |
| Circular-argument charge | Circular definition/argument, since the definition covers the entire domain, "does not even rise to bad" (outline Q&A); self-reference must be complete; mutable self-reference is forbidden | A closed loop is closure, not fallacy |
| "Does the Seventh Axiom claim absolute correctness" | Not entered on the absolute-truth list ("at minimum (0, 1)" is not extended); status = procedural optimality + commitment | Case closed |

**Self-reference and scale-freeness.** This axiom refers to itself and applies to itself — the two layers are the **final closure** of the whole system's "eternal vs. absorption" problem:

> **Scientific layer**: principle is optimality (criterion and commitment unchanged, an eternal law of science). **Text layer**: absorb the better (the "human text of science" is revisable).

**Mutable self-reference is forbidden**: what gets updated is the text-layer formulation, not the criterion itself — "optimality" must not be used to redefine "correctness" (the criterion belongs to the First Axiom), and "absorbing the better" must not cancel the criterion's stability. With this, the two-layer protocol of all seven axioms is fully unified: **the scientific layer's criteria are eternal; the text layer's formulations absorb the better.** **The axiom is scale-free**: the optimality criterion contains no scale parameter — applicable to any discipline (natural, social, formal, meta-science) and any expressive space (matter/mind neutral); whatever claims to be better passes through the same comparison procedure.

**The loop with the earlier axioms.**

| Related axiom | Relation |
|---|---|
| Zeroth Axiom | Only with boundaries are there comparable objects; theories and the Principle are both bounded existences |
| First Axiom | **Completeness is the feasibility constraint**: judge correctness first, then compare optimality; optimality does not guarantee correctness |
| Second Axiom | Theories tend toward completeness (direction); the optimal is unimprovability within the correct set (criterion); "absorb the better" is the text-layer execution of tending toward completeness |
| Third Axiom | Theory competition is a game (turbulence); the comparison procedure is the mechanism for finding the better, not the standard defining the better — the criterion lives inside the system and does not follow power |
| Fourth Axiom | The optimal theory retains correct ignorance (does not forcibly close the undecided; a conjecture is a conjecture) — optimality does not lead to omniscient hegemony |
| Fifth Axiom | Justice = efficiency (selection at the social level); the Principle = compression-optimal (selection at the cognitive level) — same framework, no mutual reduction |
| Sixth Axiom | Meaning = price; the optimal theory is the one with the highest compression efficiency under the same constraints (an engineering-layer index of meaning density) |

**Summary.** The Seventh Axiom does three things:

1. **Turns Occam's razor into a principle**: from heuristic to judgable, committable meta-principle — optimal = unimprovability under complete constraints;
2. **Realizes the system's self-judgment**: self-reference is not circular argument but a necessary requirement of the definition of optimality — the Principle submits to its own selection procedure, cases pass trial, the better is absorbed;
3. **Gives an open convergence commitment**: the Principle is not an endpoint but a convergence point — the criterion is eternal, the formulation open.

The seven axioms now close the loop:

> **Existence is difference (Zeroth) → Correctness is completeness (First) → Things tend toward completeness (Second) → Games are turbulence (Third) → Freedom is ignorance (Fourth) → Justice is efficiency (Fifth) → Meaning is price (Sixth) → Principle is optimality (Seventh)**

**Worship no authority; submit only to the optimal.**

### 3. Note on the demotion of formalization

Numerical optimizations like O(t) = E(t) − λC(t), argmax, MDL are **engineering-layer annotation sources** (precedent: "game solver = efficiency annotation source") and must not enter the definitional layer:

- **λ is an arbitrary trade-off parameter** (the strength of the complexity penalty is undefined); with λ any theory can be made "optimal" — formalization that does not trace back to axioms is mere notation;
- **Optimal is a category** (unimprovability under complete constraints, judged case by case as a binary), not an extremum of an O value;
- **argmax is notation for the comparison procedure**; comparison results are given by cases passing trial ("shop around" is the naive form).

Trustworthy deduction must be based on axioms: engineering-layer annotations serve ranking and comparison; the criterion lives in the definitional layer.

### 4. Repositioning Popper and Kuhn

**Popper**: the Principle does not recognize falsificationism (outline Q&A: philosophy of science itself no longer upholds it) — the positive demarcation criterion is the commitment/delivery dual axes. Falsifiability does not enter the optimality dimensions; the counterexample channel (counterexamples checkable, one suffices) belongs to the delivery axis and has nothing to do with falsificationism: falsification is a terminal narrative; a counterexample is a channel. **Kuhn**: paradigm shift is a sociological description (how a group switches beliefs); this axiom gives the judgment norm (how a group should select the better) — the direction is pulled by the optimality criterion; game turbulence (Third Axiom) can cause temporary misalignment; the long-term trend converges toward the criterion. Their status in the system: genealogy (the philosophical quadrant's prehistory), replaced not synthesized — same treatment as the Fifth Axiom's handling of the philosophical quadrant.

### 5. Gödel: back-reference to the First Axiom

This axiom opens no second front. The First Axiom's stance stands: **the Gödel theorems are not accepted; their deductive logic and scientific status are not recognized**; the Hilbert process plus Gödel-world minimization (G/H → 0, a **conjecture**) resolves the problem technically within a system the opponents accept. Case table (First Axiom): the Gödel sentence = a boundary-revision instruction; truth not judged. This axiom's "optimal" does not conflict with that: the optimal is unimprovability under complete constraints (judged case by case), not "everywhere provable inside a formal system" — proof is not the highest arbiter; judgment and commitment are.

### 6. Soros reflexivity (analogical note)

Reflexivity (participants' biases affect fundamentals, and fundamentals feed back into biases) is the Third Axiom's cognitive-level form — of the same type as the minority game's reflectivity (predictions change the predicted object). This axiom's connection: reflexivity manufactures short-term turbulence and bubbles; the comparison procedure (cases passing trial, the counterexample channel) is the long-term convergence mechanism — an analogical/engineering-layer note, not admitted into the definition.

### 7. Circular-argument defense (back-reference to the First Axiom's notes)

The system is self-referential and defines over the entire domain: circular definition/argument is not itself wrong, merely potentially bad — but since the Principle defines over the entire domain, it does not even rise to bad. The Self-reference theorem requires self-reference to be complete: everything the Seventh Axiom needs to judge itself (comparison procedure, absorption rule, the two layers, the case table) has been fully expressed — the loop is closure. Mind the distinction: **a closed loop is the self-consistency of a definition; mutable self-reference is the fallacy** (mutable self-reference is forbidden: the criterion does not change; formulations can absorb).

### 8. Detailed note on the absorption commitment and eternal law: the two-layer closure

The tension between "scientific principles must not change" and "absorb the better" has been handled by the two layers since the Second Axiom; the Seventh Axiom completes the closure:

| Layer | Content | Mutability |
|---|---|---|
| **Scientific layer** | The seven axioms' criteria and commitments | Unchanging (eternal law of science) |
| **Text layer** | Formulations, cases, boundaries, theorems | Absorb the better (the human text of science is revisable) |

This is the final form of the relation between "science" and "the human text of science" (outline Q&A). **What is absorbed is better formulation and content; the criteria (completeness, comparison, commitment/delivery) do not move** — otherwise "better" itself loses its standard (using mutable self-reference to cancel criterion stability = a pruning target). The Q&A's verbatim answer to "how does the Principle guarantee it won't be overturned by a future 'in-domain counterexample'" is delivered here: "**It doesn't. The Principle does not predict its own future. It only commits that if something better appears in the future, it will immediately absorb it into the Principle.**"

### 9. Mirror of the Rationality theorem

The mirror here of the First Axiom's generalization source (Rationality theorem: under complete information, behavior is determined): **with complete information, selection is determined** — the selection procedure converges, under complete information (everything needed for judgment fully expressed), to the unimprovable. "Unimprovability" thereby gains a behavioral reading: not a static property but the convergence result of the comparison procedure — isomorphic to "no best, only better" (the everyday anchor): procedural optimality = the limiting commitment of a convergence procedure.
