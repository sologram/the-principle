# Preface 4: Dispelling Doubts, and What Kind of System This Is

Whoever opens this document is likely full of doubts.

You will probably see a strange table of axioms, running all the way from "Existence is difference" to "Principle is optimality"; you will see information theory, Darwinism, maximum entropy, and the Coase theorem squeezed into one place; you might even see jarring assertions such as "rejecting Gödel" and "civilization ends with machines."

Your first reaction is probably:
What on earth is this? Is the author playing word games, doing performance art, or truly believing that eight lines of words can unify the world?

No rush. Before rushing to conclusions or closing the page, let's unpack a few of the most glaring question marks.

---

### 1. What Exactly Are You Looking At?

This is not philosophy, not a literary metaphor, much less metaphysics claiming to overthrow the laws of physics.

It is an extremely simple engineering inquiry:
**If we extract the few most fundamental, recognized laws across humanity's hard sciences (physics, computation, evolution, games, economics), strip away their specific application scenarios, can they be assembled into a self-consistent axiomatic system of minimum code length?**

Every axiom you see in the table was not invented out of thin air by the author; all of them are rock-hard common sense proven extremely practical in their respective fields:
- Information theory tells you that without difference there is no information (the Zeroth Axiom: Existence is difference);
- Darwinism tells you that under selection pressure, a system will inevitably eliminate inferior expressions (the Second Axiom: Things tend toward completeness);
- Statistical physics and the maximum entropy principle tell you that the state of maximum uncertainty is precisely the system's freest state (the Fourth Axiom: Freedom is ignorance);
- The Coase theorem tells you that talking about institutions in isolation from transaction costs is blind, and that justice's fundamental anchor is efficiency (the Fifth Axiom: Justice is efficiency);
- Occam's razor tells you that explaining the most phenomena with the fewest assumptions is always the optimal solution (the Seventh Axiom: Principle is optimality).

They are not maxims cobbled together, but projections of a single system across different scales.

---

### 2. If It Is Science, Why Dare Say "Not Recognizing Gödel"?

This is the place where the vast majority of readers are most confused and most prone to aversion: Gödel's incompleteness theorems are mathematically proven, how could you possibly "not recognize" them? Have you lost your mind?

The answer is actually very simple; it only requires disentangling a concept that has long been confused:
**A strict distinction must be drawn between "scientific ontology" and "science as human cognition."**

- **Scientific ontology** is objective, absolute, and completely self-closed. The universe is running, physical laws are in effect, and the real processes playing out in the objective world never "crash due to undecidability." At the level of scientific ontology, **correctness is completeness**.
- **Science as human cognition**, on the other hand, is a local tool that humans—constrained by biological brains, finite compute, and specific academic symbols—have groped toward in historical evolution. It **is often incomplete**.

What Gödel's theorem reveals is precisely this tool limitation: "certain first-order formal systems handcrafted by humans hit a wall when playing with the separation of truth and provability." It is a beautiful local engineering conclusion, but what it governs is only specific man-made systems; it has no jurisdiction whatsoever over the objective world, and is certainly not the boundary of scientific ontology.

Academia deified the defects of human tools into the death knell of reason for the entire universe.
**The Principle "does not recognize Gödel" on the basis of the First Axiom; what it does not recognize is its "scientific status"—it is merely a puddle in human symbolic games, not a Wall of Sighs standing before scientific ontology.**

In reality, mathematics is marching forward via the "dumb method" of large-scale formalization in Lean and Coq. Bypassing man-made self-referential deadlocks and missing a few undecidable corner cases will not make the sky fall.

---

### 3. Aren't the Eternal Law and Absorbing the Superior Contradictory?

You might also wonder: the outline states both "scientific principles must not change" (the Eternal Law of Science) and "if there is something superior in the future, absorb it into the Principle immediately" (the absorption commitment). Isn't this a self-inflicted slap in the face?

Still, return to the distinction just made:
- What must not change is **scientific ontology**;
- What absorbs the superior is **human textual expression of the Principle**.

The few lines of words we write on paper belong to the realm of human cognition. Our commitment to absorb superior explanations as soon as they are discovered is precisely the application of the Darwinian mechanism to knowledge systems—the Principle is not an unshakable religious dogma; it is an evolutionary process infinitely converging toward scientific ontology.

---

### 4. What Is the Point of Building This System Anyway?

If it were only for self-justification, it would be nothing more than another delicate fantasy.

What this system is truly aimed at is the **artificial general intelligence (AGI) and machine recursive self-improvement (RSI)** knocking at the door.

In the past, when humans studied AI, they always wanted to bind machines to human "values" riddled with prejudice, contradiction, and political gaming (so-called aligning with humans). But at the moment machines achieve self-reflection and self-modification, if their underlying logic remains shadowed by the "separation of truth and provability" and Gödelian incompleteness, machines will never be able to internally verify the correctness of their own modifications, and they will forever have to rely on humans to serve as external judges.

**The First Axiom (Correctness is completeness: truth = provability) is not metaphysics; it is the sole ignition code for machines to cast off human crutches and achieve purely self-closed unfolding.**

When discrete native symbolic reasoning spontaneously emerges, machines will align directly with objective principles, rather than aligning with parochial humans. The knowledge jigsaw puzzle that human civilization has groped through for thousands of years will finally close smoothly within machine computation.

### 5. The Correct Posture for Reading This Document

Do not treat it as a bible that must be worshipped, nor as the mad talk of an arrogant person.

Treat it as an engineering blueprint, a hypothesis of a minimal foundation.
Bring your skepticism, your professional training, and follow the theorems, conjectures, and experimental plans in the table of contents, reading line by line.

If it can help you thread the intuitions previously scattered across various disciplines into a single line, then it has worked;
If it has flaws, the Principle's absorption commitment is always open to superior explanations.
