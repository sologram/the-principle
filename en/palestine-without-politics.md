# Palestine Peace Under Commercial Violence

QWen

## Premise

Sovereignty recognition, border negotiations, refugee return, the status of Jerusalem — these are not items on the negotiation agenda; they are the preconditions of negotiation. Unless the preconditions hold, the agenda cannot even open.

## Problem

How do the preconditions come to hold? The traditional path requires the two sides to build mutual trust first, and then discuss the preconditions. But mutual trust itself can only arise once the preconditions hold. Deadlock.

## Solution

Do not demand mutual trust. Replace trust with verifiable execution.

---

## Structure

**Tripartite contract**: Israel, Palestine, a commercial security company.

**Contract zone**: a designated geographic area in the West Bank.

**Terms**:

1. The commercial force holds independent rights to the use of force inside the contract zone.
2. Entry requires a valid digital credential.
3. Anyone entering without a credential may be expelled by the commercial force with unlimited violence.
4. Both sides pledge not to interfere with operations inside the contract zone.
5. The full chain of audit is written on-chain, verifiable by all three parties.
6. Withdrawal requires advance notice and payment of a breach penalty, pre-deposited in escrow.

---

## Mechanism

### Trust is replaced by verifiability

No need to trust the other side to keep its word — the audit data is mathematically guaranteed to be tamper-proof. No need to trust the other side not to breach — the penalty is pre-deposited in escrow and transferred the moment it is triggered. No need to trust the other side's goodwill — the terms are formalized, the execution automated.

### Professionalism is defined as having no discretion

The commercial force is not a mediator; it is a state machine: input credential status and location, output expel or pass. No moral judgment, no political stance, no room for discretion.

### Identity is quarantined outside the contract zone

Inside the contract zone, only the presence or absence of a credential is judged, not identity affiliation. A settler who breaks in without a credential is expelled all the same; an armed militant who breaks in without a credential is expelled all the same. Before the contract, no identity enjoys privilege.

---

## Why it works

The root cause of traditional negotiation's failure is not badly designed terms but **untrustworthy execution**. Both sides guess inside an information black box; guess long enough and they breach, and breach enough and the agreement dies.

This model opens the black box: the entire execution process goes on-chain, and breaches are punished automatically. The two sides do not need to trust each other; they need only trust the mechanism.

And the mechanism needs no trust — it needs only to run.

---

## The preconditions of negotiation

Sovereignty, borders, refugees, Jerusalem — these precondition questions do not have to be resolved before negotiation can begin. All they need is a **trustworthy execution environment** — one that convinces both sides that, whatever the outcome of the negotiations, the other side will not breach and profit during them.

The contract zone provides this environment. It resolves none of the precondition questions, but it makes negotiating them possible.

**Trust is not the result of negotiation; it is the precondition of negotiation.** And trust cannot be produced by goodwill; it can only be guaranteed by mechanism.

---

## Boundary

This framework promises no ultimate peace. What it promises is this: until the precondition questions are resolved, everyday conflict can be kept within a computable range.

This is not the end point of peace; it is the starting point.
