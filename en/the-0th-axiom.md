# The Zeroth Axiom of Existence

## Text

**Zeroth Axiom: Existence is difference.**

> Whatever existence enters this system is bounded by difference; only what is bounded by difference enters this system.

This fixes what "things" are in the system:

> **Existence = distinguishability = having a boundary = expressibility.**

The three are synonymous: difference is content, boundary is form, expression is presentation.

**Expressive space.** Expression is the presentation of a difference structure. An expressive space is the domain in which difference structures can be presented — linguistic, mathematical, and physical forms all qualify. Without difference, the space itself is not distinguishable and therefore not expressible — so the expressive space itself is subject to this axiom. Expressibility is the **object side**'s completeness of expression; the **criterion side**'s completeness of expression is the First Axiom (completeness = everything required for the judgment has been expressed). The two form a dual: existence = completeness of expression on the object side, completeness = completeness of expression on the criterion side.

**The position of cognition.** Cognition is a component of existence. It belongs to the difference structure and does not stand outside existence. Distinguishability is a relation among structures within the structure; it does not depend on the presence of a knower.

**Things and boundaries.** A thing becomes a thing because it has a distinguishable boundary between itself and "everything else." Without a boundary there is no thing; if the boundary does not close, the thing mixes into other things; if the boundary is unstable, the verdict drifts along with it.

**Decision rule.**

> If x has no difference from every other structure whatsoever, including the background, then x does not constitute the subject term of this system.

Equivalently: the subject-term position already demands distinguishability. An x that can be mentioned and negated is itself already a difference structure.

**Non-existence = error (wrongly existing).** This identity holds only for what can be mentioned: "does not exist" is a verdict on a mentionable structure, not an assertion about the unmentionable. It is not metaphysical void but boundary failure:

| Failure | Description | Manifestation |
|---|---|---|
| No boundary | Not separated out from the background | No distinguishable difference from the background |
| Boundary leakage | Mixes in other things, boundary does not close | Attribution cannot be stably delineated |
| Boundary confusion | Boundary is blurry and drifting | The verdict is unstable |

So this axiom does not assert "all existence is correct"; it asserts only: all existence presents itself as difference, and a considerable portion of it is erroneous existence. How this is adjudicated is executed by the First Axiom; once adjudicated, it is not pursued further (Extraterritorial enforcement theorem).

**Jurisdiction.** This axiom holds adjudicative jurisdiction over every candidate (before the court) — it renders a verdict on whether any given candidate is a subject term, and even the excluded receive a verdict; the extraterritorial is not a failure of jurisdiction but one of the possible verdicts of jurisdiction. **Universal adjudication, limited pursuit**: The Principle does not pursue non-existence deeply, and therefore does not pursue erroneous things deeply either (Extraterritorial enforcement theorem). Pruning and disposal belong to the engineering layer — the Principle is the courthouse, and order beyond the courthouse still requires dispatching large numbers of police officers, or even armies. The Zeroth Axiom adjudicates only "what it is"; the First Axiom adjudicates "whether it is right" and carries the burden of recognition.

**Self-reference and scale-freeness.** This axiom refers to itself and applies to itself; axioms are scale-free — the decision rule contains no scale parameter, and the scale is given by the boundary itself. The full treatment of self-reference (including paradox cases) is carried by the First Axiom (Self-reference theorem).

**Commitment and absolute truth.** This axiom has two layers in one. **The scientific layer**: this axiom is an absolute truth, not conditioned on human understanding or acceptance (Absolute truth theorem (0, 1)). **The textual layer**: this axiom is stated in the form prescribed by the entrance, and advanced as a scientific commitment — it commits to correctness and bears the burden of delivery. Accept it, and you discuss by "existence is difference"; refuse it, and you do not enter this expressive space, your domain of things is not bounded by this axiom, the truth of this axiom is not thereby changed, and this system does not pursue it further (Extraterritorial enforcement theorem).

## Notes

### 1. Existence is not "having," it is "being distinguishable"

If a thing has no difference from anything whatsoever, it does not form a distinguishable structure: it cannot be located, cannot be named, cannot be measured, and cannot enter any expression. So "existence" here is not the everyday "having" but: **it has formed a distinguishable structure in the expressive space.** Note that this is a structural statement, not an epistemological review: difference does not require the presence of a knower, only that two structures be separable from one another.

The lexicon of language corroborates this choice: everyday speech names defects with "loopholes, gaps, limits, vagueness" — all boundary words. "Existence speaks in terms of boundaries" was long ago written into the naive lexicon; the boundary is not a frame imposed by theory.

### 2. Cognition is a component of existence

Cognition is not a mirror reflecting existence; it is a difference structure inside the field. Three direct consequences:

- **The unity of error**: misrecognition, conceptual drift, and confusion of adjudication are all boundary failures internal to cognition, of the same kind as external failures, and therefore fall into the same category of "wrongly existing";
- **Attribution of marginal categories**: fictions and mathematical objects are difference structures inside the expressive space of cognition — they have differences, have boundaries, are expressible, and are admitted on the same footing, with no need to posit a separate type of existence;
- **The theory's self-applicability**: The Principle itself is an expressive structure, bounded by difference, and must therefore hold of itself.

### 3. The scalarity of adjudication: boundary is scale

Adjudication is always done on a specified scale; bivalence is the result of selecting a scale, not something absolute. A boundary below that scale is not "not yet distinguishable" but simply does not close on that scale and has not yet come into being as a boundary. **Boundary is scale**: a distinguishable difference specifies for itself the scale on which it closes — this pairs with the scale-freeness of the axiom, so the scale is never borrowed from the adjudicator.

### 4. Information is an alias

"Existence is information, information is difference" is an equivalent restatement, not a new entity: **information = the distinguishable presentation of difference**. It is a perspective, not a third term lying outside the system.

### 5. Quantum identical particles do not refute it

Particles being identical does not mean there is no difference. What actually carries distinguishability is not the "individual particle" in the classical sense, but the field, the state, measurement relations, and symmetry structure. Identical particles can be discussed precisely because they still reside in a distinguishable physical expressive space: differences persist between wavefunction, quantum number, and measurement outcome. "Identical" means only that certain observables share an eigenvalue, not that there is "no difference" — a quantum state is itself a difference structure. This axiom therefore still holds at the quantum level.

### 6. What this axiom does in the system

- **Sets admission**: it admits on the single condition of being distinguishable, bounded, and expressible, and holds adjudicative jurisdiction over every candidate, with a verdict even for the extraterritorial;
- **The starting point of meta-science**: it takes over the problem domain of metaphysics by meta-scientific means — it settles at one stroke the "absolute existence" that has no differences, ruling it inadmissible as a subject term and declining to docket the case. This is not evasion but the disposition of "correct metaphysics";
- **Paving the way for the First Axiom**: the Zeroth Axiom supplies "things" and holds adjudication; the First Axiom adjudicates "correct" and bears the burden of recognition (erroneous existence, extraterritorial cases, self-reference and paradox); the Second Axiom, "things tend toward completeness," supplies the direction;
- **Supporting the Boundary theorem**: the Boundary theorem follows directly from "things are determined at the boundary";
- **Adjudicates, does not pursue, does not punish**: pursuit stops at the Extraterritorial enforcement theorem, punishment stops at the scientific secularism law.

### 7. Entrance provisions and absolute truth: layered, not mixed

This axiom has two layers in one: as **science**, it is absolute truth; as **text**, it is an entrance provision. This is the relationship between "science" and "the human text of science" — science does not change, text can be revised. Hence "commitment" and "absolute truth" do not conflict: commitment occurs at the textual layer (the procedure for entering expression plus the scientific commitment), while absolute truth holds at the scientific layer (not awaiting acceptance). The problem is not that one may not speak of absolute truth; it is that the two layers may not be spoken of as one. The defense of the textual layer is a posteriori: counterexamples can be checked (one is enough), and a better version is absorbed; the scientific layer needs no defense, being definitional.

### 8. Meta-science: correct metaphysics

The Principle's self-positioning is **meta-science, that is, correct metaphysics**: metaphysics (problem domain: existence, truth, the most general structures) + science (method: there is commitment, there is delivery) = meta-science. "Correct" is not a rhetorical flourish; it is the First Axiom's "correct = complete = everything required for the judgment has been expressed" — metaphysics in which everything required for the judgment has been expressed.

Anti-philosophy opposes the philosophical **quadrant** (no commitment, no delivery, semantic drift, evasion of responsibility) and the philosophical **method** (passing off uncertainty as profundity); it does not oppose the metaphysical problem domain. By the love-of-wisdom boundary law: the passage from uncertainty to certainty is the boundary between philosophy and science — metaphysics, once it has obtained a determinate criterion, crosses over and becomes meta-science; by the love-of-wisdom freedom law: philosophy has no right or wrong, while The Principle does have right and wrong (right = complete), and therefore The Principle is not philosophy.

The expressive space is neutral with respect to matter (weak expression) and mind (strong expression): "existence is difference" distinguishes between neither; both are distinguishable structures. This is the basis of the ultimate goal, "unify all of science: natural, social, formal, meta-science" — meta-science, in its identity as the general theory of expressive structures, supplies the criterion framework for the other three quadrants.

### 9. Summary

1. **Sets admission**: it holds adjudicative jurisdiction over every candidate, with a verdict even for the extraterritorial;
2. **Supplies the structural perspective**: existence is difference, and information is an alias of the same thing; together with the First Axiom it forms the dual of completeness of expression;
3. **Paves the way for what follows**: without existence there is no correctness; without difference there is no boundary; without boundary there is no thing; once adjudication is established, recognition belongs to the First Axiom, pursuit is limited, and punishment belongs to the secular.
